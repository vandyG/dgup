Findings

•  The observed instances of excess gas usage support our hypothesis that
temperature plays a significant role in driving gas consumption patterns.

•  Seasonal and weekly trends are evident across several usage variables.

•  Usage-2 represents the largest facility, which explains its higher scale of

consumption compared to the others.

•  Usage-2_1 is hypothesized to be an additional meter within the same facility, likely
supporting heating operations during winter months. The decline in consumption
after 2021 is likely attributable to the shutdown of certain buildings.

•  The anomalies observed in December 2017, and early January 2018 are

hypothesized to be the result of meter malfunction.

•  Analysis of the Delivery vs. Usage gap is planned for inclusion in the second half of

the project.
